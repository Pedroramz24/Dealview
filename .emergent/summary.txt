<product_requirements>
The project is a map-first commercial real estate (CRE) CRM with a dark, glass-morphism UI. The core goal is to provide tools for deal management, contact organization, and team collaboration.

Key product initiatives implemented or in progress:

1.  **Email Campaigns:** A crown jewel feature allowing users to send bulk emails. It includes a Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) model for SendGrid, a drag-and-drop Unlayer email builder, contact segmentation with tags, and scheduling. **Status: Paused by the user due to complexity.**

2.  **Settings Page:** A fully functional, multi-section settings hub with an enterprise-grade UI. It consolidates Profile, Account, Security, and Data & Privacy. Key features include avatar upload, profile data management (name, title, phone), password changes, viewing active sessions, and a secure account deletion flow.

3.  **Team Collaboration System (Multi-Phase):**
    *   **Phase 1 (Complete):** Foundational system for team creation, sending/revoking shareable invitation links, and managing team members with roles (Owner, Admin, Agent, Viewer). Users can create and belong to multiple teams.
    *   **Phase 2 (In Progress):** Enhancing collaboration by showing team-wide performance statistics (e.g., total pipeline value) on a dedicated Performance tab within the Team page, adding a Team Deals tab to view all shared deals, enabling deal sharing and assignment from the  page, and adding a map layer to visualize team deals.
</product_requirements>
